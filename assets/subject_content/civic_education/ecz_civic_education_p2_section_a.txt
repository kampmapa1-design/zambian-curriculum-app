Model answer: Over-the-counter substances are drugs that can be bought freely without a doctor's prescription (e.g. common painkillers), while controlled substances are restricted by law because of their potential for abuse and can only be obtained with a prescription or special authorisation.

ECZ Civic Education Paper 2 (Section A), 2017 (July), Question 8: A person or an independent agent who links clients seeking insurance from an insurance company is known as ... Model answer: An insurance broker (or insurance agent).

ECZ Civic Education Paper 2 (Section A), 2017 (July), Question 9: Mr Kalibekantu was beaten at a market after a marketer shouted, "thief" in his direction. The police arrested him and kept him in police cells for two days before releasing him. Which one of his rights was violated? Model answer: His right to personal liberty -- the Constitution requires an arrested or detained person to be brought before a court without undue delay (generally within 24 to 48 hours) and informed of the reason for arrest; holding him for two days without charge or court appearance violated this right.

ECZ Civic Education Paper 2 (Section A), 2017 (July), Question 10: Mr Chilangamulilo a business man of Matebeto Village died intestate. He left K50,000 in his ZANACO Account. How much money will be allocated to the children? Model answer: Under Zambia's Intestate Succession Act, children are entitled to 50% of the estate, so the children would be allocated K25,000 of the K50,000.

ECZ Civic Education Paper 2 (Section A), 2017 (July), Question 11: What is the effect of civil wars on the environment? Model answer: Civil wars damage the environment through destruction of farmland and infrastructure, deforestation, pollution from weapons and fuel, and displacement of people onto fragile land.

ECZ Civic Education Paper 2 (Section A), 2017 (July), Question 12: Explain the concept sexual harassment. Model answer: Sexual harassment is unwanted sexual advances, comments, or conduct directed at a person that creates an intimidating, hostile, or offensive environment.

ECZ Civic Education Paper 2 (Section A), 2017 (July), Question 13: Why is Budgeting important for National Development? Model answer: Budgeting is important for national development because it helps government allocate limited resources efficiently, prioritise development projects, control spending, and promote accountability in the use of public funds.

ECZ Civic Education Paper 2 (Section A), 2017 (July), Question 14: How is poverty associated with the negative aspect of Human Development? Model answer: Poverty is linked to the negative side of human development because it limits people's access to education and healthcare, shortens life expectancy, and reduces overall quality of life -- all factors that lower a country's Human Development Index.
