Sub – Topic –Types of Books of Prime Entry – Sales Day Book
Introduction
The Sales Day Book is one of the Books of Prime Entry where we record goods sold on credit.
It is also known as a Sales Journal.
The source document used to write up the Sales Day Book is known as the Duplicate Invoice.
Specific Competence
 Record Business Transactions in the Books of Prime Entry – Sales Day Book
 Post Business Transactions to the Ledger.
KEY TERMS
 Sales – refers to goods sold.
 Credit Sales – these are goods sold on credit to customers.

Sub Topic – Types of Books of Prime Entry - Sales Returns Day Book
Introduction
The Sales Returns Day Book is a Book of Prime Entry that is used to record goods sold on credit
but later returned by our customers due to various reasons e.g., wrong colour, faulty goods,
damaged goods etc. It is also known as Sales Returns Journal, Returns Inwards Journal or
Returns Inwards Day Book. It is important because it helps to account for goods returned. The
Source Document used to write up the Sales Returns Day Book is known as the Duplicate Credit
Note.
Specific Competence
 Record Business Transactions in the Books of Prime Entry – Sales Returns Day Book
 Post Business Transactions to the Ledger.
KEY TERM
Sales Returns – refers to goods that were earlier sold but have been returned by the
customer for various reasons such as wrong colour, damaged goods, expired goods etc.
