The Religious Education Teaching module aims to provide teachers with the tools, knowledge,
and strategies needed to foster a deep understanding and appreciation of religious education
among learners.
As teachers, you play an essential role in guiding young minds towards ethical and moral
development, while nurturing their spiritual growth and cultural awareness. This module is
designed to support you in creating a learning environment that encourages critical thinking,
respectful dialogue, and a holistic exploration of religious beliefs and practices.
By engaging with the content and activities within this module, you will be equipped to, develop
learners’ competencies in understanding and analysing religious concepts, encourage empathy
and respect for diverse religious perspectives, facilitate meaningful discussions that promote
ethical reasoning and moral decision-making and create inclusive and supportive classroom
environments that celebrate diversity and foster a sense of belonging.

Topic: 1.5 COMPETITION, CO-OPERATION AND TRUST
Introduction
Competition, cooperation and trust are part and parcel of society. In the vibrant modern world,
mature competition, co-operation and trust should help to ensure that individuals and groups
compete in different types of competitions. They should also co-operate, trust and work together,
leading to the development of society at large. This chapter will provide a comprehensive overview
of the important values, attitudes and skills prompted through competition, co-operation and
trust, laying the foundation for competence-based education for a Form One Learner. As future
citizens who will be involved in various forms of competition, need to co-operate with other
people, need to be trusted and to trust others for various reasons, it is important to gain an in-
depth understanding of various aspects of competition, co-operation, and trust that are at work.
Through a series of theoretical lessons and practical lessons, learners will develop critical
competences that will serve them throughout their lives. Teachers can play a very important role
in shaping the minds of leaners, guiding them to understand and appreciate the importance of
competition, co-operation and trust, and the values and attitudes that go with this topic. They will
learn to apply critical thought, correct attitudes, and make informed decisions about competition,
co-operation and trust.

Hook
Imagine you are the captain of your school football team competing for the Inter-Schools
Cup. Your school is doing very well and your team has qualified to play in the semi-
finals of the competition the following day. Your sports teacher and fellow team members
cannot wait for the match. It is 10:00 hours on the day of the match and the two teams
are on the field. The referee, a teacher from another school, blows the whistle for the start
of the match. Your team is dominating the game. Thirty minutes into the match, your star
striker is clearly fouled inside the box and everyone on your side jumps in anticipation
of a penalty kick, which you should take as captain. However, the referee waves play on.
All of you cannot believe it and your team mates surround you, suggesting that the team
should protest the referee’s decision by walking out of the game. How will you handle the
situation?

SUB-TOPIC 1.5.1 COMPETITION AND CO-OPERATION
Introduction
There are different forms or types of competition at different levels and various ways in which
people co-operate and collaborate with one another in different situations. In this sub-topic, the
teacher will focus on exploring with the learners the importance of competition and co-operation
in society. The teacher will further focus on exploring the importance of collaboration in both
competition and co-operation in society.
This sub-topic will therefore focus on discussing the benefits or advantages and disadvantages of
competition and co-operation in society. It will further focus on examining why collaboration is
necessary in both competition and co-operation. Understanding the benefits or advantages and
disadvantages of competition and co-operation will not only enable learners to develop positive
attitudes and values towards competition and co-operation but also help them to become better
competitors, co-operators and collaborators as citizens in society after school.

SUMMARY
Key Points
 Competition exists in every society and takes place between individuals and groups
of people. There is competition in sports, education, business, work, politics, and
beauty and dressing,
 Competition has both positive and negative effects on society, such as improving
performance and high productivity, and promoting differences and division among
competing people, respectively.
 Co-operation takes place in various ways between individuals, groups and
communities, usually in order to achieve a common goal or what is good for
everyone.
 Co-operation also has both positive and negative effects on society, such as
achievement of community projects and laziness among some members of the
group or community, respectively.
 Although collaboration goes more closely with co-operation, it is important for
