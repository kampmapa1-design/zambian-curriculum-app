*3. THE PROFIT RULE*
The owner's words (OWNER): for every $10 of ALL costs and obligations, including taxes, the business should make $10 or more in NET profit.

In plain terms: net profit must be at least equal to total costs including taxes. That means revenue must be at least 2 times total costs, or a net margin of at least 50% of revenue.

"Taxes" can be read two ways and the difference is large. PLEASE CHOOSE.

Reading A: taxes are a share of the payment (VAT taken out of a VAT-inclusive price, or a turnover tax).
AI budget per unit = price x (1 - 2 x channel fee - 2 x tax share) / 2 - infrastructure

Reading B: income tax at rate "tau" on profit.
Total operating costs must be at most: price x (1 - 2 x tau) / (2 x (1 - tau))

*6. AI MARKING ECONOMICS*

*6.1 The three engines (from the code)*
All three run through one Cloud Function, gradeMarkingScriptConcise. Names below are as the teacher sees them under Scan Marker.

1. CONCISE MARKING. Model: gemini-3.6-flash. "Pure AI": it reads the paper's own rules, identifies the questions, and marks from subject knowledge. It may also receive question-paper images (up to 10, re-sent with EVERY script) and an optional reference key. Output: per-question marks PLUS page number and bounding box (so ticks and crosses can be drawn on the photo), a rubric, and 3 to 8 observations.

2. STABLE MARKER. Model: gemini-3.5-flash-lite (cheaper, about 4 seconds vs 16 to 20). Same pure-AI approach on a lighter model. Output: marks only, NO boxes, plus rubric and observations.

3. UPLOADED MARKING KEY BASED MARKING. Model: gemini-3.6-flash. Strictly bound to a marking key the teacher uploaded. The key text is sent with each call. Output like Concise, including boxes.

Also present but not modelled separately: an older scheme-based path (gradeMarkingScript, economically similar to Key-based) and one-off marking-key creation from a question paper. Home Assignment marking of pupil photos reuses Concise and Stable.

*6.3 How a call is billed*
- Images: Google's default for Gemini 3 models is 1,120 tokens per image (low 280, medium 560, high 1,120, ultra-high 2,240). VERIFIED for "Gemini 3 models". Google's page does NOT confirm it for the 3.5 and 3.6 models, so we treat it as ASSUMED. The older method (258 tokens per 768 x 768 tile, about 6,192 tokens for a 3000 x 4000 photo) is kept as the "Heavy" scenario.
- Thinking is ON by default and billed as output. gemini-3.6-flash defaults to "medium" and gemini-3.5-flash-lite to "minimal". (VERIFIED) The app sets NO thinking level anywhere, so it pays the default.
- Retries: the server tries a call up to twice (a second attempt without the JSON schema if the first fails or is unreadable), and the marking queue retries a failed script once more. Every retry re-sends and re-bills all images. Modelled as 1.05 to 1.25 times (ASSUMED). Real failure rate UNKNOWN.

*6.9 COST LEVERS, RANKED BY LIKELY IMPACT*
Each needs testing against marking accuracy.
1. Lower the thinking level for marking. Output is about 80% of cost at Base and thinking is the largest single unknown.
