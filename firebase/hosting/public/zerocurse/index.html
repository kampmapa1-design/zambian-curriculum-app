ZeroCurse

Remove swear words from your videos. ZeroCurse finds the swear words and bleeps or mutes them, so you can share a video with family, school or church. By default your video is cleaned on your own device.

What it does

Bleep or mute

Choose a bleep or silence over every swear word. The picture is never touched, only the sound at those exact moments.

Catches what others miss

It listens to the whole video twice and then re-listens to every cut, so quick, mumbled or slurred words are caught too.

Your rules

Pick how strict it is, and add your own words to always censor or to never censor, such as a name that sounds like a swear word.

Private by design

Your video file never leaves your device. If you choose faster processing for long videos, only the speech audio is sent, and you can keep everything on your device instead.

Long videos and films

Handles hours of video. You can pause, stop, or close the app and carry on later from where it stopped.

More on Windows

Hawk Eye lets you read every word, listen and fix cuts by hand. Pro adds reports, subtitles, a batch queue and fast cloud speed.
