ZeroCurse Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026. Applies to the ZeroCurse app for Android and the ZeroCurse app for Windows, and the ZeroCurse pages of this website. ZeroCurse is made by PhiloSoft Ventures ("we", "us").

The short version

  • Your video stays on your device. We never upload a video file.
  • Videos up to 10 minutes are cleaned entirely on your device and nothing is sent to us.
  • For longer videos, the app can send only a small audio file to our cloud service so it can be processed faster. You can switch this off ("keep it on this device"). The audio is deleted when the job ends.
  • The Windows app has an optional account (Google sign-in) for the free trial, Pro and purchases.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not use your audio to train anything.

What the app does with your video

ZeroCurse listens to the sound of a video to find swear words, then bleeps or mutes those moments. The cleaned video is saved on your device (in your Downloads folder, in a folder called "Cleaned Up by ZeroCurse"). Recognition runs on your device. The app needs access to the video you choose and permission to run in the background while it works.

When audio leaves your device

Only when a video is longer than 10 minutes and you have not chosen to keep it on your device (and, on Windows, only with a Pro plan or free trial): the app extracts a speech-only audio file (the picture is never included) and sends it over an encrypted connection to our cloud service. The service finds the swear words and returns only the times to censor, plus the transcript of the speech, which is used only for the result screen and the optional diagnostics report. The uploaded audio is deleted as soon as the job ends, and any result is deleted within about an hour. If you add your own words to the always-censor list, the job stays on your device.

Diagnostics reports

The app can create a diagnostics report (settings, timings, and a transcript of the speech that was heard) to help us fix a problem. It is created on your device and is only sent if you choose to share it.

Adverts

Videos up to 10 minutes are free in exchange for watching short adverts. On Windows, adverts are shown only while you are online and only on the free plan. Adverts are supplied by a third-party advertising service, which may collect device and usage information under its own privacy policy. Pro users see no adverts.

Who we share information with

We use these service providers to run ZeroCurse: Google (Google Cloud and Firebase, for hosting, sign-in and our database; Google Play for Android payments), and, for Windows payments, our payment provider (which acts as seller and handles tax and receipts). They process data only to provide those services. We do not sell your information and do not share it for their own marketing.

Where it is processed, and security

Our cloud service runs in data centres operated by Google. Connections are encrypted. The database cannot be reached directly from the apps or the website; only our service can read or write it, and licences are digitally signed. No system is perfectly secure, but we design ZeroCurse so that it holds as little about you as possible.

Your choices and rights

  • Keep everything on your device: turn on "Keep it on this device" (Android) or use videos of 10 minutes or less.
  • Delete your data on your device by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.
  • Windows account: email us to see or delete your account data. Deleting the account ends your Pro plan and unused credits.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights over your information (access, correction, deletion, objection). Email us and we will respond.

Children

ZeroCurse is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Parents who use it to clean videos for children are our intended audience.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and its date. Material changes will also be shown in the app.
