Smart Teacher

AI-assisted lesson planning and marking support for Zambian teachers — plan lessons, mark work, and run your school, built for real Zambian classrooms, and works even without reliable internet.

Join Early Access

What it does

Lesson Plans & Schemes of Work

Generate complete lesson plans and schemes of work aligned to the official Zambian curriculum — both Grade 10-12 and Form 1-4 — offline, with AI assistance when you're online.

Chief Marker — AI-assisted marking

Mark scripts by camera instead of by hand. Chief Marker reads handwritten answers against your own marking key and gives you a graded, reviewable result in minutes, plus results analysis across the whole class.

Timetable Generation

Build a full school timetable — periods, subjects, teacher constraints and all — from plain-language input, in minutes instead of a weekend.

Admin Tools

Convert Word documents to PDF, turn a recorded meeting into structured minutes, and transcribe handwritten documents into editable Word files — directly from your phone.

Student submissions

Pupils photograph and submit handwritten assignments or tests straight from their own phone. Teachers receive a clean, organized PDF — no printer or scanner needed.
