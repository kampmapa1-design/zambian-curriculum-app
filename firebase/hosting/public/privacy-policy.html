Privacy Policy — Smart Teacher

Last updated: 28 August 2026

Smart Teacher is an offline-first curriculum companion app for Zambian teachers. This policy explains what data the app handles and why, in plain terms.

What the app stores on your device

Smart Teacher's syllabus content, lesson content, and marking data are stored locally on your device (in a local SQLite database), not on our servers. This is intentional — the app is designed to work fully offline. We do not have access to this local data.

Account sign-in

Signing in uses Firebase Authentication. We store the identifier your sign-in method provides (e.g. an email address) to associate your account with app features that require sign-in, such as AI-assisted content generation. We do not sell or share this with third parties, and do not use it for advertising.

AI-assisted features (teaching notes, slide outlines, syllabus tools, AI-assisted marking)

Some features send content to Google's Gemini API through our own server-side function (Firebase Cloud Functions) to generate a result — for example, syllabus context to generate teaching notes, or photos of a student's answer script to generate a first-pass grading suggestion for a teacher to review. This content is sent only when you actively use that feature, is used to produce the response you asked for, and is not used to build an advertising profile. AI-assisted marking is always a first-pass suggestion — a teacher reviews and confirms every mark before it is final.

Camera access

The app requests camera access only for features that need it — capturing pages of a student's answer script for AI-assisted marking. Captured images are used for that feature and are not collected for any other purpose.

What we don't do

  • We don't run analytics or advertising SDKs that track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't require an internet connection for core offline features (browsing syllabi, lesson content).

Data retention and deletion

Local app data stays on your device until you clear it (e.g. by uninstalling the app or clearing app data in your device settings). Account sign-in data is retained while your account is active; contact us to request deletion.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if the app later adds advertising or additional cloud features — we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
