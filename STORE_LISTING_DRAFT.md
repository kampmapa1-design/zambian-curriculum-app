## Full description

**Smart Teacher — the Zambian Curriculum Companion**

Smart Teacher helps Zambian teachers plan lessons, generate schemes of work, and produce teaching notes — for both the current Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) and the Outcome-Based Curriculum (OBC) — without needing an internet connection for the core work.

**Works offline, where it counts**
Everything you generate — lesson plans, schemes of work, teaching notes, and records of work — is built entirely on your device, from real, sourced curriculum content. No signal in your classroom or staffroom? No problem: plan your week on the bus, at home, or anywhere else, and it just works.

**What you can generate**
• Lesson Plans — matched to CBC or OBC structure automatically, resumable if you're interrupted mid-lesson
• Schemes of Work — pick a subject, grade/form, and term; get a properly formatted scheme, ready to share
• Teaching Notes & Slides — bulletin, essay, or PowerPoint format, generated from the actual syllabus for your topic
• Record of Work — weekly or fortnightly, pulled automatically from what you've already taught

**Share instantly**
Every document exports as an editable Word file or PDF, shared straight from the app through WhatsApp, email, Bluetooth, or Google Drive — no extra apps, no manual formatting.

**Real curriculum content, honestly sourced**
Lesson plans, schemes of work, and teaching notes are grounded in real Curriculum Development Centre (CDC) syllabus documents and Teaching Modules — not generic filler. The app also gives you direct access to the CDC's own digital library of official Teaching Modules, syllabi, and ECZ past papers, right from your phone.

**Built for how Zambian classrooms actually work**
Smart Teacher was built specifically around Zambia's own curriculum structures — Form 1 through 4 for CBC, Grade 10 through 12 for OBC — not adapted from a generic template.

Smart Teacher is actively developed, with new subjects, features, and curriculum content added regularly.
